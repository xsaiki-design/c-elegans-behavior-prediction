What do the names mean?

Names like AVA or RME aren't abbreviations: they're labels given to each neuron type when the worm's nervous system was first mapped (White et al. 1986). A final L or R means the left or right cell of a pair; D or V means dorsal (back) or ventral (belly). Roles below: the highlighted types in plain words, then every type's category from the Cook et al. 2019 wiring study.

Both models watch the same real worm and score every moment for "reversal coming". Alarm: each model sounds an alarm on its own riskiest 20% of moments, so both get the same number of alarms (a fixed cut-off like 50% would be unfair, because the two models score on different scales). Warned: a real reversal counts as warned of if an alarm was on in the 5 seconds before it started, and at least as early as you choose below. False alarm: an alarm with no reversal in the next 5 seconds. Example: the worm reverses at 1:00 and the alarm came on at 0:57. That is a warning 3 s ahead, so it counts under every setting.
